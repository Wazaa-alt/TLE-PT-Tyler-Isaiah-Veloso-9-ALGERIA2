Our Business Proposal

Business Name

Cream Parfait

Business Description

Cream Parfait is a refreshing dessert made with fruits, syrups, ice cream, and cream. It combines different flavors and textures to create a balanced and enjoyable dessert.

Target Customers

Our target customers include students, young people, families, and older customers who enjoy refreshing and customizable desserts.

Main Attraction

Our main attraction is the combination of colorful fruits, creamy ingredients, ice cream, and syrups. The appearance of the product makes it appealing to customers.

My Individual Contribution

Marketing

I helped develop marketing strategies for Cream Parfait and focused on attracting young customers.

Target Audience

I helped identify our target audience, especially students and young people who are active on social media.

Social Media Promotion

I helped plan social-media strategies to introduce Cream Parfait to potential customers.

Free Taste

One of our marketing strategies was to offer free samples so customers could try the product before buying.

My Reflection

Working on our TLE activities helped me understand the importance of teamwork, communication, creativity, preparation, and planning.


During PT1, making dango with cream taught me how preparation and presentation can affect the appearance of a food product. Presenting the product in a fancy cup also showed me how presentation can make food more appealing.


During PT2, I learned more about how a business is planned and promoted. My role in marketing helped me understand how businesses identify their target customers and use social media to attract them.


Overall, these activities helped me improve my teamwork, creativity, communication, and responsibility.
